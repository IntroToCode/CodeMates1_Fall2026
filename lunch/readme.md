# Tomato Bisque Soup 

## Description

It's from Ladle & Leaf on campus. And it's a warm, tasty and specially amazing during Fall

### Ingredients:

- [ ] 2 tbsp butter
- [ ] 1 tbsp olive oil
- [ ] 1 medium yellow onion, chopped
- [ ] 3 garlic cloves, minced
- [ ] 2 tbsp tomato paste
- [ ] 28 oz (800 g) canned organic whole peeled tomatoes, with juices
- [ ] 2 cups vegetable broth (gluten-free)
- [ ] 1 tsp sugar
- [ ] 1 tsp salt
- [ ] 1/4 tsp black pepper
- [ ] 3/4 cup heavy cream

### Steps for preparation:

1. Melt the butter with the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onion and cook for about 7 minutes, until soft but not browned.
2. Stir in the garlic and tomato paste. Cook for 2 minutes, until the paste turns a darker red.
3. Add the tomatoes, broth, sugar, salt, and pepper. Break up the tomatoes with a spoon. Bring to a boil, then lower the heat and simmer for 20 minutes with the lid half on.
4. Take the pot off the heat and blend until completely smooth. If using a regular blender, fill it only halfway and hold the lid down with a towel.
5. Return the pot to low heat and stir in the cream. Warm for 3 minutes without letting it boil.
6. Taste and adjust. Add a pinch of sugar if it is too sharp, or a pinch of salt if it tastes flat. Serve hot.

### Notes:

- This is a copycat of the Organic Tomato Bisque from Ladle & Leaf, which does not publish its recipe.
- For a smoother texture, pour the blended soup through a fine sieve before adding the cream.
- Keeps for 4 days in the fridge. Reheat gently so the cream does not split.
